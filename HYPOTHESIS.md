# Altcoin Regime Research

## Objective
Predict periods when altcoins outperform their market beta, particularly when high-beta short positions are vulnerable.

## Research questions
- Can we predict altcoin outperformance over the next 3–10 days?
- Does the position within the 3-month or 6-month price range help?
- Does relative performance across Top 30, 40 and 100 universes predict future returns?
- Can these signals improve portfolio sizing and reduce drawdowns?

## Research process
Claude proposes hypotheses and develops the research.
GPT independently reviews and challenges the results.
Experiments must be reproducible and evaluated out of sample.
